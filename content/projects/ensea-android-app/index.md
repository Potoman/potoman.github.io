Built and maintained over several years (2007-2013) during and after my time at ENSEA (École Nationale Supérieure de l'Électronique et de ses Applications), this Android application became well known and widely used among students at the school.

It was one of my first real exposures to shipping and supporting software actually used by a community, long before Android app development became part of my day job (later at Parrot Faurecia Automotive, working on AOSP services).
