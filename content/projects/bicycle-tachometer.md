My very first electronics project, built in 2004, long before my engineering studies: a homemade tachometer for a bicycle.

It's the earliest example of the curiosity that eventually led me into embedded systems and electronics as a career — figuring out how to sense, measure, and display something in the physical world with simple, hand-built hardware.
