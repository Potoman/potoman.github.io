A small Windows tool built to help color-blind people identify colors on screen — pointing at any pixel and getting back a clear color name/value, rather than having to guess.

A simple accessibility-focused side project, born out of wanting to solve a real, concrete problem for someone.

**Source:** [github.com/Potoman/ColorBlind](https://github.com/Potoman/ColorBlind)
